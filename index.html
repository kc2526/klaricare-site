Why we exist

Dosing errors are one of the most common medication problems in children, and most happen at home: the wrong measuring cup, a dose based on age when it should be based on weight, two products that both contain acetaminophen, or a 2 a.m. dose that no one remembers giving. None of these are careless parents. They are the predictable result of confusing labels, tired caregivers, and information scattered across bottles, discharge papers, and memory.

We build tools that put the pediatrician's checklist into the parent's hand at the moment it matters: the right dose for this child's weight, in the syringe you actually own, with a record everyone in the household can see.

Weight-based, not age-based

Children's doses depend on weight. Every calculation starts there, with the concentration printed on your bottle.

Safety checks built in

Minimum age, spacing between doses, and 24-hour maximums across every product that shares an ingredient.

Curated by clinicians

Dosing rules and guides are written and reviewed by practicing pediatricians against current labeling and guidelines.
